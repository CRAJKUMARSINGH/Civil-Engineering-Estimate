Yes 👍 that’s the **classical centerline method** you are describing. Let’s formalize it:

---

## 🔹 Centerline Method Logic

### 1. **Room Sizes Input**

From your form → every room has **Length × Width**.

### 2. **Wall Thickness Input**

* Main wall thickness (already captured from PCC / superstructure width).
* Partition wall thickness.

### 3. **Total Centerline Length**

$$
\text{Total CL} = \sum \Big[ 2 \times (L + W) \Big] \;+\; \big(\text{Wall Thickness Adjustments}\big)
$$

But we must **avoid double-counting** at junctions and deduct partition overlaps.

---

### 4. **Practical Formula for Excel**

For each room `i`:

$$
CL_i = 2 \times (L_i + W_i) - \text{Partition Wall Deductions}
$$

Total:

$$
CL_{main} = \sum CL_i - CL_{partition}
$$

Where:

* `CL_partition` = Sum of partition wall lengths × (wall thickness adjustment).
* This ensures **common walls are not counted twice**.

---

## 🔹 Implementation in Excel

Let’s say:

* Room table starts at row 41 (`C` = Length, `D` = Width).
* Partition wall total length = `Inputs!B121`.

We can compute **Main wall CL** at `Inputs!B123` with a formula like:

```excel
=SUMPRODUCT(2*(C41:C50 + D41:D50)) - B121
```

(where `C41:C50` are lengths of rooms, `D41:D50` are widths).

# 🔹 PLASTERING

### 1. **25 mm plaster (internal, main walls)**

$$
\text{Qty} = \text{Main Wall CL} \times \text{Room Height}
$$

* Formula in Excel:

```excel
=Inputs!B123 * Inputs!B7
```

---

### 2. **25 mm plaster (external, main walls)**

$$
\text{Qty} = \text{Main Wall CL} \times (\text{Room Height} + \text{Plinth Height} + 0.15)
$$

* Formula:

```excel
=Inputs!B123 * (Inputs!B7 + Inputs!B11 + 0.15)
```

---

### 3. **20 mm plaster (partition walls, both sides)**

$$
\text{Qty} = 2 \times \text{Partition CL} \times \text{Room Height}
$$

* Formula:

```excel
=2 * Inputs!B121 * Inputs!B7
```

---

### 4. **Ceiling plaster**

* Same as **floor area of each room**.

$$
\text{Qty} = \sum (\text{Length} \times \text{Width})
$$

* Formula:

```excel
=SUMPRODUCT(C41:C(40+RoomCount), D41:D(40+RoomCount))
```

---

# 🔹 SHUTTERING

We compute **surface areas** for each RCC component:

### 1. **Columns**

$$
\text{Shuttering Area} = \text{Perimeter of Column} \times \text{Column Height}
$$

* For `n` columns:

```excel
=SUMPRODUCT(ColumnPerimeterRange, ColumnHeightRange)
```

---

### 2. **Beams**

$$
\text{Shuttering Area} = 2 \times (\text{Beam Depth} + \text{Beam Width}) \times \text{Beam Length}
$$

---

### 3. **Lintels**

* Per opening (door/window):

$$
\text{Area} = \text{Width} \times \text{Thickness} \times 2 \; \text{(sides)}
$$

---

### 4. **Sunshades (chajjas)**

* Simple rectangle:

$$
\text{Area} = \text{Projection} \times \text{Width}
$$

---

### 5. **Slab (staircase & floors)**

* Slab shuttering area = **Plan area of slab**
* Staircase shuttering = (Tread × Riser × Steps × Width)

---

# 🔹 RCC (Concrete + Steel)

### 1. **Concrete Volume**

* For each RCC component:

  * **Column**: Base area × height
  * **Beam**: Cross-section × length
  * **Slab**: Thickness × slab area
  * **Staircase**: Tread × rise × no. of steps × width

---

### 2. **Reinforcement Steel (Sariya)**

* Typically taken as % of concrete volume (empirical, unless Struds table is read).
* Eg:

  * Columns → 2.5% of RCC volume
  * Beams → 2%
  * Slab → 1%
  * Stairs → 1.5%

In **statutory measurement books (MB)** under **PWD / CPWD / HSR**, every quantity must be shown in **detailed measurement lines** (Length × Breadth × Height = Quantity), and only then summed up into the **abstract total**.

So instead of writing:

```
Earthwork in excavation = 15 cum
```

We must show something like:

```
Earthwork in excavation in foundation trenches  
1.   5.0 × 0.6 × 1.0 = 3.00 cum  
2.   5.0 × 0.6 × 1.0 = 3.00 cum  
3.   4.0 × 0.6 × 1.0 = 2.40 cum  
4.   4.0 × 0.6 × 1.0 = 2.40 cum  
5.   2.0 × 0.6 × 1.0 = 1.20 cum  
6.   2.0 × 0.6 × 1.0 = 1.20 cum  
7.   2.0 × 0.6 × 1.0 = 1.20 cum  
Total = 14.40 cum (say 14.40 cum)
```

This applies to **all HSR items** — brickwork, RCC, plaster, flooring, shuttering, etc.

### 1. **Detailed Measurement Sheet (MSR / MB style)**

# 📏 Key Building Levels (Concepts & Use in Estimation)

### 1. **Average Ground Level (AGL / GL)**

* **Definition**:
  The **mean level of the natural ground surface** around the proposed building, before any excavation or filling is carried out.
  Measured by taking reduced levels at several points around the plot and averaging them.
* **Purpose**:

  * Serves as the **reference zero** for foundation depth calculation.
  * Earthwork quantities (excavation, filling) are computed with respect to this level.
* **Estimation Role**:

  * Excavation depth = **(Founding Level – AGL)**
  * Filling quantity = (Plinth Level – AGL) × Area

---

### 2. **Founding Level (Foundation Level)**

* **Definition**:
  The level at which the **bottom of the foundation** rests.
  Usually taken **below AGL** depending on soil strata and structural design.
* **Purpose**:

  * To transfer load safely to the bearing strata.
  * To avoid soil settlement and ensure stability.
* **Estimation Role**:

  * Excavation depth = **(Founding Level – AGL)**
  * Foundation concrete (PCC, footing, etc.) quantities measured from this level upward.

---

### 3. **Plinth Level**

* **Definition**:
  The finished floor level of the ground floor, **above ground level**.
  Generally **0.45 m to 0.60 m above GL** in residential buildings.
* **Purpose**:

  * Protects building from rainwater ingress, dampness, flooding.
  * Defines the **datum for superstructure measurements** (walls, flooring, doors, windows).
* **Estimation Role**:

  * Plinth filling = (Plinth Level – AGL) × Floor Area
  * Superstructure wall height measured from Plinth Level to First Floor Level.

---

### 4. **Floor Levels**

* **Ground Floor Level (GFL)**:
  Coincides with Plinth Level (finished floor inside building).

* **First Floor Level (FFL)**:
  Height measured from Plinth Level to the **top surface of first floor slab**.
  Typical storey height = **3.0 m – 3.6 m (residential), 4.0 m+ (public buildings)**.

* **Successive Floor Levels**:
  Repeated storey heights stacked above FFL.

* **Estimation Role**:

  * Wall height = Floor-to-floor level – Slab thickness.
  * RCC beams/columns/slabs computed between floor levels.
  * Plastering and painting areas depend on floor heights.

---

### 5. **Parapet Level**

* **Definition**:
  The level up to the top of the **parapet wall** above the terrace.
* **Purpose**:

  * Provides safety, architectural finish.
* **Estimation Role**:

  * Parapet wall masonry = Parapet length × thickness × height
  * Plastering (internal & external) computed separately above terrace.

---

### 🔹 Example in MB (Measurement Book)

Suppose:

* AGL = 100.00 m
* Founding Level = 98.50 m (i.e., 1.50 m below AGL)
* Plinth Level = 100.45 m (i.e., 0.45 m above AGL)
* First Floor Level = 104.65 m (i.e., 4.20 m above Plinth)

Then:

* **Excavation depth** = 1.50 m
* **Plinth filling depth** = 0.45 m
* **Wall height (Plinth → First floor)** = 4.20 m

All these will be **coded row-wise** in the Measurement Book before totals are written.

| **Skirting**                      | `Σ(Room Perimeter × Skirting Height)`                    | Perimeter, default h=0.1 m | m²                |
| **Dado**                          | `Σ(Room Wet Wall Length × Dado Height)`                  | Length, default h=2.1 m    | m²                |
| **Doors (per row)**               | `No. × Width × Height`                                   | n, w, h                    | m²                |
| **Windows (per row)**             | `No. × Width × Height`                                   | n, w, h                    | m²                |
| **Door/Window Masonry Deduction** | `(Opening Width × Opening Height × Thickness)`           | Opening size, wall t       | m³                |
| **Granite Jambs/Sills**           | `Perimeter × Thickness`                                  | per opening                | m² / m³           |
| **RCC Columns**                   | `Σ(Col. Cross-section × Height)`                         | b, d, h                    | m³                |
| **RCC Beams**                     | `Σ(Beam Cross-section × Length)`                         | b, d, l                    | m³                |
| **RCC Slab**                      | `Slab Thickness × Slab Area`                             | t, area                    | m³                |
| **RCC Stairs**                    | `Σ(Tread × Riser × Width)` per flight                    | steps, width               | m³                |
| **Shuttering (Columns)**          | `Perimeter × Height`                                     | per column                 | m²                |
| **Shuttering (Beams)**            | `2 × Depth × Length` (sides) + `Width × Length` (bottom) | d, w, l                    | m²                |
| **Shuttering (Slabs)**            | `Slab Area`                                              | area                       | m²                |
| **Shuttering (Stairs)**           | `Flight Area + Riser Area`                               | geometry                   | m²                |
| **Parapet Wall Volume**           | `Parapet Length × Thickness × Height`                    | l, t, h                    | m³                |
| **Terrace Area**                  | `Σ(Room Sizes) + Σ(Wall Thickness additions)`            | room sizes, walls          | m²                |
| **Terrace Plaster**               | `Terrace Area × Thickness`                               | area, t                    | m³                |
| **Paint/Finish Area**             | Same as plaster areas (internal/external/ceiling)        | plaster area               | m²                |

def excavation_volume(agl, fl, foundation_area):
